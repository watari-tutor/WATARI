## ✨ What can you do with WATARI?

WATARI is designed around the way students actually study: plan what matters, work on weak areas, practise, revise and track progress.

### 🧠 AI-powered study tools

- 💬 **Talkable Chat** — Ask questions and interact with your AI study companion.
- 🎯 **Doubt Solver** — Get help understanding concepts and solving academic doubts.
- 📝 **Short Notes** — Generate structured study notes, including handwritten-style note images.
- 🃏 **Flashcards** — Create interactive flashcard decks for active recall.
- 🧩 **Quiz Lab** — Generate and attempt interactive quizzes, including multiple-choice and integer-answer questions.
- 🗺️ **Interactive Concept Maps** — Explore topics through connected concepts, formula callouts and important warnings.
- 📎 **Attachments** — Add supported images and documents to AI study requests.
- 🎙️ **Voice features** — Use supported voice input and spoken AI responses.
- ▶️ **YouTube study context** — Give WATARI a supported YouTube video link to use available video transcript content as context.

### 📚 Study planning and revision

- 🛣️ **Roadmap** — Organise your complete syllabus in prerequisite order, from the first topic to the last.
- 📌 **Backlog** — Keep track of weak topics that need extra attention.
- 🚦 **Priority levels** — Organise backlog topics as High, Medium or Low priority.
- 🔍 **Weak-area tracking** — Identify whether you need help with concepts, theory, numericals, practice questions, specific subtopics or a full chapter.
- 🧠 **Mistake Bank** — Keep track of mistakes and use them to guide revision.
- 🔁 **Revision workflow** — Turn weak areas and quiz mistakes into revision tasks.
- 📅 **Exam Control** — Manage exams and their dates with an exam calendar.
- ✅ **To-do List** — Keep your study tasks organised.

### ⏱️ Productivity and progress

- 🍅 **Focus timer** — Run focused study sessions and record your progress.
- 🔥 **Study streaks** — Track consistency across study days.
- 🌱 **Forest Island** — Grow your study forest as part of the focus experience.
- 🏆 **XP and achievements** — Earn experience through study activity and quizzes.
- 🥉 **Ranks and levels** — Progress through Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum and Diamond tiers.
- 🦉 **AI avatars** — Choose an avatar for your profile.
- 😴 **Sleep log** — Record your sleep.
- 🏫 **Attendance tracking** — Track attendance and departure times separately for configured institutions such as school, college and coaching.

## 🛣️ Development Roadmap

WATARI is a work in progress. The goal is to keep improving it based on real student feedback.

### ✅ Already implemented

- Daily sleep and attendance prompt flow.
- Exam calendar and exam-date management.
- Syllabus Roadmap with prerequisite ordering.
- Backlog with priorities and weak-area tracking.
- Manual, Roadmap-based and AI-assisted backlog creation.
- Interactive quizzes and quiz-result review.
- Mistake-to-backlog and revision-task workflow.
- Mastery quizzes for completed backlog topics.
- AI-generated short notes and interactive concept maps.
- Image and document attachments for AI study tools.
- Profile, XP, achievements, ranks and selectable avatars.

### 🔜 Planned and upcoming

These are development goals, not promises that the features are already available:

- 🤖 **Smarter WATARI Agent** — A more capable conversational agent that can help navigate the app and carry out requested study actions with appropriate confirmation.
- 🧠 **Adaptive Personalisation** — More personalised explanations and practice based on learning history, with clear student control over saved data.
- 🌲 **Interactive Forest Island** — Zoom, pan, place and move trees in a more interactive study environment.
- 👥 **Social Study Challenges** — Study challenges with friends, shared streaks and progress comparisons.
- ☁️ **Improved Cloud Sync** — Better multi-device synchronisation, conflict handling and clearer sync status.
- 🔔 **Smart Notifications** — Configurable reminders for focus sessions, quizzes and revision.

Want something added? Let me know. Your suggestions can help shape what WATARI becomes next. 💜

For the detailed development plan, see [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md).

## ⚙️ AI setup

Some AI-powered features require a Gemini API key.

1. Open WATARI.
2. Open the app settings/menu.
3. Add your own Gemini API key where requested.
4. Save the setting and try an AI feature.

Keep your API key private. Do not post it in GitHub issues, comments, screenshots or public messages. API usage may be subject to Google's terms, quotas and billing settings.

## 🔐 Privacy and data

WATARI stores study information locally on the device. The app also includes cloud-sync functionality. When using AI or sync features, information may be sent to the relevant external services to provide that functionality.

- Do not enter passwords or other sensitive information into AI prompts.
- Keep your Gemini API key private.
- Review the permissions and prompts shown by Android.
- Check the app's behaviour and the privacy implications of any feature before using it with personal information.
